Personal gws

Personal gws is a private tool used only by its owner (kanjirou0301keitai@gmail.com) to access their own Google Workspace data (Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Sheets, Docs) from their own computers via the gws CLI and Hermes Agent.

個人が自分のGoogleアカウントのデータを自分のPCから操作するためだけに使う非公開ツールです。
